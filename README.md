# Superstore Sales Performance & Regional Analysis Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard_preview.png)

## 📌 Executive Summary
This interactive Power BI dashboard provides an end-to-end analysis of retail sales performance, tracking **$2.30M** in total revenue and **$286.40K** in total profit across multiple regions and product segments. The dashboard is designed to help executive stakeholders quickly evaluate sales trends over time, monitor key financial metrics, identify top-performing product sub-categories, and assess geographic distribution.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Addressed
* **Revenue & Profitability:** What is the overall sales volume ($2.30M), net profit ($286.40K), and total discount impact across operations?
* **Geographical Distribution:** Which states and regions drive the bulk of sales volume, and where are performance bottlenecks located?
* **Customer Segmentation:** How is total sales revenue distributed among Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments?
* **Product Performance:** Which product sub-categories generate the highest sales volume versus underperforming items?
* **Historical Trajectory:** What do multi-year sales trends reveal regarding seasonality and peak order periods?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformations:** Power Query (data cleaning, column profiling, type conversion)
* **Visualizations & Features:**
  * **Card Visuals:** High-level KPI tracking (Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount).
  * **Area / Line Charts:** Temporal sales distribution across order dates.
  * **Donut & Pie Charts:** Customer segment split and regional sales share.
  * **Funnel Chart:** Sub-category performance ranking.
  * **Filled / Point Map:** State-level geographical sales distribution.
* **Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset (Retail transaction records).

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Business Recommendations
1. **Dominant Segments:** The Consumer and Corporate segments generate the overwhelming majority of revenue, indicating marketing resources should focus on retention campaigns for these high-value brackets.
2. **Category Concentration:** Phones and Chairs lead product sales volume significantly, while sub-categories like Fasteners, Labels, and Envelopes contribute marginally to overall volume.
3. **Discount Management:** Monitoring high-discount items is essential, as elevated discount rates directly correlate with margin erosion in underperforming regions.
